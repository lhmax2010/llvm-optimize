[Part 4] packaging: harden the native archive converter

Validate compressed section headers before using their declared sizes
as relocation bounds. Accept the known zlib and zstd types and require
zero or power-of-two alignment. Require zero-width relocation markers
other than R_X86_64_NONE to lie inside their target section, and reject
RELR sections in archive members.

Log inherited architecture options and signal handler states at startup.
Malformed diagnostic environment flags remain nonfatal. Conversion
commands, existing summary fields and existing log lines are unchanged.
