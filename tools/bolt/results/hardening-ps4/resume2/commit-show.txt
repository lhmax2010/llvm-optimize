    packaging: harden the native archive converter
    
    Validate compressed section headers before using their declared sizes
    as relocation bounds. Accept the known zlib and zstd types and require
    zero or power-of-two alignment.
    
    Require zero-width relocation markers other than R_X86_64_NONE to lie
    inside their target section, and explicitly reject RELR sections in
    archive members.
    
    Log inherited architecture options and signal handler states at startup.
    Keep malformed diagnostic environment flags nonfatal, and preserve the
    existing conversion commands, summary fields and log lines.
