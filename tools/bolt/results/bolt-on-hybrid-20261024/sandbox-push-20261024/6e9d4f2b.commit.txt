cmake: use hybrid LLVM linkage on x86_64

Enable shared LLVM and Clang linkage in the x86_64 recipe while keeping
clang, lld and llvm-ar statically linked to LLVM components. Their
driver aliases continue to share the same executable. Other tools use
libLLVM and, where applicable, libclang-cpp through the normal upstream
interfaces.

Use TIZEN_HYBRID_LINK and a per-consumer TIZEN_STATIC_LLVM property to
select component dependencies only for the three opted-in drivers. When
disabled, the shared-link interfaces retain upstream behavior. Keep
BUILD_SHARED_LIBS and the unified LLVM driver disabled on x86_64.

Preserve upstream forced-static llvm-tblgen, clang-tblgen, lldb-tblgen,
llvm-config and llvm-exegesis. Tool-private archives and build utilities
retain upstream behavior. Do not change armv7l or aarch64 recipes,
optimization flags, static-devel packaging or native archive conversion.

Validation: CMake generation with the complete x86_64 recipe parameters;
all-static, hybrid and shared controls; unchanged ARM build/install
macro expansions; clean patch application. Full build and RPM/runtime
validation are left to the receiving team.
