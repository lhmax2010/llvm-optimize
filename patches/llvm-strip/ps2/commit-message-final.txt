packaging: use llvm-strip for x86_64 archives

The platform builds with LLVM. Use llvm-strip for static archive
post-processing in this package while keeping executable stripping on the
platform default. Override __strip_install_post only to change the
brp-strip-static-archive argument, without overriding __strip. Check that
llvm-strip is available after native archive conversion.

This depends on change 356627: bitcode must first be converted to native
objects. Keep find-debuginfo and other RPM post-processing intact. The
override is copied from tizen_macros:34-38 in rpm-build-4.14.1.1-1.4 and
must track future platform macro changes. The disabled comment-note step
is documented outside the macro body. The override covers every static
archive in the package buildroot, including compiler-rt subpackages.

The combined x86_64 candidates completed a full LLVM 22.1.8 build through
one serial -ba --noprep continuation. Full-spec expansion and exec/exit
tracing establish that conversion preceded stripping, all 270 archive
llvm-strip calls exited zero, debug-enabled brp-strip was not executed,
and find-debuginfo and the remaining enabled steps ran normally.

All 225 development archives have native members, no DWARF and complete
indexes. All 45 compiler-rt archives retain their build originals'
allocated contents, symbol attributes, relocations and indexes; stripping
may change non-allocated ELF structure. Host and Tizen GNU ld and lld
consumers passed, including ASan, UBSan, profile and builtins probes.
Independent installation and same-tree SKIP checks also passed.

The same-environment unpatched non-archive installation comparison is
pending because its disk admission budget was not met; do not infer
byte-identical non-archive outputs from a different snapshot build.
Earlier small RPM fixtures covered debug on/off and confirmed executable
stripping remains on the platform default in the non-debug branch.

Limit this override to x86_64. ARM follows after archive conversion and
packaging validation on those targets.
